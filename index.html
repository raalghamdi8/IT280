How to study

How each week page works

Before the lecture

  • Skim the week page: read the outcomes and the summary.
  • Note anything you want explained in class.

After the lecture, or if you missed it

  • Read the page top to bottom. It is written to be learned on its own.
  • Use every “Try it” demo and answer every “Check yourself” question before opening its answer.
  • Take the self-marking quiz at the end.

In the lab

  • Follow the lab section step by step. Each step says where to click and what you should see.
  • Labs are 90 minutes of work, then 30 minutes of support time. If you are stuck, stay and the instructor will help you one-to-one.
  • Tick the “Before you submit” list, then submit your repository link on Blackboard.

Arabic support

  • Tap any key-term chip to see the Arabic term.
  • Each week ends with a searchable English–Arabic glossary. Use the English terms in exams.

Policies

Using other people’s work and AI tools

Licences and credit

Use another person’s image, font, sound or model only if its licence allows your use. Credit every asset with Title, Author, Source and Licence, and record it in your asset log. Week 1 explains how.

Generative AI

You may use AI tools, and you must always declare how in your AI-use log. AI output may not replace a part the brief says you must make yourself. Undeclared use is an academic integrity case.

Tools

Krita or GIMP and Inkscape (2D), Audacity (audio), DaVinci Resolve (video), Blender 4.2 or later (3D), Python with VS Code for OpenGL and GLSL (Week 10), Unity 6.3 LTS (interactive), Git and GitHub. All are free to install at home. Some labs also need free accounts: GitHub (Week 1), a personal Adobe ID for Mixamo (Week 14) and, optionally, Sketchfab (public models only on the free plan).
